Privacy & Data Governance

Privacy Policy

Last Updated & Effective Date: September 2026 | The Data Pilot (thedatapilot.in)

1. Introduction & Scope

The Data Pilot ("we", "us", or "our"), operating via https://www.thedatapilot.in, is dedicated to safeguarding your personal data and privacy. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, process, store, and protect your information in compliance with the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, and applicable international data standards.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to evaluate your admission eligibility, process your enrollments, deliver course materials, and provide placement assistance:

  • Personal Contact Data: Full name, email address, WhatsApp/mobile number, city/state, and educational or professional background provided through consultation forms.
  • Transaction & Billing Identifiers: Razorpay payment order IDs, transaction reference IDs, dates, and amounts paid. (Note: We do NOT store sensitive financial credentials like raw credit/debit card numbers, CVVs, or UPI MPINs; these are processed directly by our regulated payment aggregator).
  • Technical & Analytical Insights: Browser type, device category, IP address, and referral sources to optimize website responsiveness and performance.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your data is processed strictly for legitimate educational, academic, and administrative purposes:

  • Processing course admissions, batch onboarding, and issuance of certificates.
  • Delivering live lecture access links, class recordings, datasets, and curriculum updates.
  • Providing personalized career mentorship, resume evaluations, and interview preparation.
  • Dispatching official admission payment receipts and transaction acknowledgments.
  • Complying with statutory accounting and taxation audits.

4. Third-Party Payment Gateway Integration (Razorpay)

All online financial transactions on thedatapilot.in are routed through our authorized payment partner, Razorpay Software Private Limited ("Razorpay").

  • When you make a payment, your payment details (cards, netbanking credentials, UPI handles) are encrypted and handled directly by Razorpay under strict PCI-DSS (Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard) Level 1 compliance.
  • The Data Pilot receives only transaction status updates, amounts, and unique transaction reference codes to confirm your admission.
  • For more details on Razorpay’s security infrastructure and privacy practices, please review the Razorpay Privacy Policy.

5. Data Protection, Security & No-Sale Guarantee

We deploy robust SSL/TLS 256-bit encryption for all data transmissions. We maintain strict role-based internal access controls ensuring that only authorized admissions personnel can access candidate profiles. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party telemarketers or advertisers under any circumstances.

6. Data Retention & Your Rights

We retain student records for the duration of the course enrollment and career support lifecycle, or as required by law for financial accounting records. You have the right to request access to your personal data, seek corrections to inaccurate contact information, or request account data erasure by writing to our compliance team.
